Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers "n8n Email AI Agent", a private automation operated by Pulin Chaudhari (Bengaluru, India).

What the app accesses

The app connects only to the Gmail account of its owner, through Google OAuth. It sends emails written by the owner and reads messages in that account (such as bounce notices and replies) so the owner's own workflows can track them.

How data is used and stored

  • Data from Google APIs is used only to run the owner's own email workflows.
  • It is processed on the owner's own n8n instance and is not sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored encrypted in the owner's n8n instance and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
